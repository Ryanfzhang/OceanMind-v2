# Ocean Question And Scale Framing

## when_to_use
Use when a question is still broad, mixes scales, or could mean different oceanographic objects or processes.

## research_objective
State the phenomenon, region, time window, vertical range, target variable, and whether the object is a basin, shelf, coast, front, eddy, water mass, station, or transect.

## questions_to_resolve
- What is observed, inferred, hypothesized, or merely requested as a visualization?
- Which competing mechanisms could produce the same pattern?
- What scale mismatch, boundary condition, or seasonal phase would make an interpretation misleading?

## evidence_requirements
Keep exact dataset, paper, selection, and figure refs separate. Record what resolution and temporal coverage can distinguish the alternatives.

## process_checkpoints
Name the intended comparison, counterexample, and uncertainty source before calling a result a conclusion.

## expected_artifacts
ProjectContextArtifact, ObservationArtifact, ClaimArtifact, HypothesisArtifact, InteractiveView, or ReportArtifact as appropriate.

## quality_gates
Do not collapse correlation into mechanism. Do not silently substitute a regional mean for a point, section, or water-mass question.

## stop_or_escalation_conditions
Ask for a bounded region, period, depth convention, or scientific target when those choices materially change the answer.
